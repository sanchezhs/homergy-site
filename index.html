homergy
Home energy cost watcher

Keeps an eye on home energy costs

homergy is a small self-hosted tool. It compares the cost of a household's electricity and gas contracts with the public offers listed by the Spanish energy regulator, and only sends a notification when switching is worth it.

Self-hostedRuns on its owner's own server.
Read-onlyReads invoice emails; never sends or changes anything.
PrivateNo data is shared with third parties.

It is a personal project, used only by its owner. It is not offered as a service.
