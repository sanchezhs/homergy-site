Privacy policy

homergy is a personal tool used only by its owner, on their own Google account.

What it accesses

  • Read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, delete or modify email.
  • Only the invoice emails from the owner's energy retailer are read, to extract the invoice summary: dates, consumption and amount.

How data is handled

  • Data is stored on the owner's own server.
  • It is not shared with, sold or sent to any third party.
  • No analytics or tracking.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
