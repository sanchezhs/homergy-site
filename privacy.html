Privacy policy

homergy is a personal tool used only by its owner, on their own Gmail account.

  • It requests read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, delete or modify email.
  • It only reads invoice emails from the owner's energy retailer, to extract the invoice summary (dates, consumption, amount).
  • The data is stored on the owner's own server. It is not shared with, sold or sent to any third party.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
